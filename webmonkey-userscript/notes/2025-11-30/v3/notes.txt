conclusions and new strategy:
=============================

  * URLs that begin with:
      /series/*
    1. extract <VIDEO-ID> for every episode in every season from window.__REACT_QUERY_STATE__
    2. rewrite DOM with list of deep links to each SxxEyy:
      /tv-shows/<VIDEO-ID>

  * URLs that begin with:
      /tv-shows/<VIDEO-ID>/*
      /movies/<VIDEO-ID>/*
    1. extract <VIDEO-ID> from URL pathname
    2. extract HLS video URL from window.__REACT_QUERY_STATE__
       where:
          const page = window.__REACT_QUERY_STATE__.queries[N].state.data.pages[M]
          (page.id === VIDEO_ID) && (page.type === 'v') && Array.isArray(page.video_resources)
